🎯 How to Play

Goal: score 10 hits before the 60-second timer ends.

🖱️ Aim: drag from the gulel/pouch and release to throw. The dotted arc shows the predicted path.

🎯 Throw: wait for the 2-second reload between throws. You can make up to 30 throws per level.

🏃 Runner: the character moves left and right and may crouch, jump, or block. Watch the AI hint while charging.

💩 🧻 🍅 🪨: each object has slightly different flight behavior. The fifth slot is your custom uploaded object.

🔥 Streak: consecutive hits build your streak. Misses and blocks reset it. Your best streak is saved.

🔓 Levels: get 10 hits to unlock the next, faster level. Progress and per-level bests are saved in this browser.

⌨️ Keyboard: use the available aim/fire controls on desktop; touch/drag controls work on phones and tablets.

⏸ Game Paused

Take a break. Your timer, runner and throws are paused.
